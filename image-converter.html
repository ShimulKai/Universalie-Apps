1. Overview & Privacy Commitment

Image Converter is an offline, standalone image transformation and compression tool published by Universalie Apps. The application is built with a strict zero-data-handling philosophy:

  • No Internet Permission: The application does not declare or request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. It has zero technical capability to connect to the internet, make network calls, or transmit any data.
  • Zero Data Collection: We do not collect, store, transmit, process, or sell any personal data, usage metrics, or images.
  • 100% On-Device Processing: All image conversions, format encodings, compressions, crops, rotations, watermark applications, and PDF document generations execute entirely on your device hardware.
  • No Advertising or Analytics SDKs: The application contains zero advertising frameworks, no tracking SDKs, no device advertising ID (AD_ID) collection, and no third-party telemetry.

Summary: Your images never leave your phone. The app operates completely isolated from the web, ensuring absolute privacy for your personal, family, and professional photographs.

2. How Your Images Are Handled

  • Volatile Memory Processing: When you pick one or more images, they are read into volatile device RAM solely to render the live before/after preview and execute your requested transformations.
  • Temporary In-Memory Cache: To guarantee smooth scrolling through selected images, low-resolution preview thumbnails are kept in an in-memory LRU cache during the active session. This cache resides in RAM and is completely destroyed when the app process ends.
  • Exporting Files: Converted output files are saved strictly to local device storage in user-accessible standard locations (such as Pictures/ImageConverter via the Android MediaStore) or a destination folder chosen by you.
  • EXIF Metadata: You have full control over image metadata. You can choose whether to preserve original camera EXIF tags or strip them completely during export.

3. Device Permissions & Access

Image Converter utilizes privacy-preserving Android platform APIs and requires no sensitive system privileges:

API / Access Purpose Data Handling
Photo Picker / Storage Access Framework User-initiated selection of photos for conversion. System-level picker. Grants read access exclusively to the specific images you select. The app cannot browse or access the rest of your photo gallery.
MediaStore / Scoped Storage Saving converted files into the Pictures/ImageConverter directory. Standard Android Scoped Storage. Creates new files in the designated public Pictures folder. Cannot read, modify, or delete any external photos outside of images created by this app.
INTERNET Network communication. Not requested. The app does not possess network capability and cannot access the internet under any circumstances.
AD_ID / Analytics Advertising and analytics tracking. Not requested / None. The app does not track users or contain any third-party tracking libraries.

4. Third-Party Services

Image Converter integrates zero third-party services. There are no analytics providers (no Google Analytics, no Firebase Crashlytics), no ad networks, no cloud storage integrations, and no social media SDKs. The app is completely self-contained.

5. Data Retention & User Control

  • File Management: You maintain full ownership and control over all exported images on your device. You can view, share, or delete them anytime using your preferred gallery application or file manager.
  • Uninstallation: Uninstalling the application deletes all internal application cache from your device while preserving any exported files you saved to your device's Pictures/ImageConverter folder.

6. Children's Privacy

Because Image Converter does not collect, store, or transmit any information from any user, it does not collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Pause: Focus & App Blocker

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

Pause: Focus & App Blocker (com.universalieapps.pause) is a friction-based focus timer and mindful app blocker published by Universalie Apps. Your privacy is foundational to how the app works:

  • 100% On-Device Operation: All app-blocking logic, focus timers, schedules, and insights run locally on your Android device.
  • Zero Data Collection or Servers: We do not operate backend servers, user accounts, cloud tracking, or analytics SDKs.
  • Zero Advertising SDKs: The app contains no advertisements, no ad networks, and no Advertising ID (AD_ID) collection.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All preferences and focus records are stored exclusively in your device's private application storage (SharedPreferences) and never leave your device:

  • Guarded App Rules: The list of apps you choose to guard, along with your chosen friction mode (Mindful Breathe, Intention Check, Cognitive Math, or Strict Lock), pause duration, unlock allowance, and daily soft limits.
  • Focus Sessions & Schedules: Active focus timer state, daily focus minutes, focus streaks, and recurring focus or wind-down schedules.
  • Friction History & Notes: Local logs of friction events (app name, timestamp, whether you resisted or unlocked, and any optional intention note you typed) as well as your optional Personal "Why" Reminder.
  • Temporary Unlocks: Short-lived expiration timestamps for apps you intentionally unlocked. Expired timestamps are automatically pruned.

3. Device Permissions & Prominent Disclosure

Pause requests only the Android permissions strictly necessary to detect when a guarded app is launched and display your mindful friction gate:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS
(Usage Access)		 Detects in real time when a user-selected guarded app moves to the foreground. Evaluated strictly in memory on-device. Your broader app usage history is never recorded, profiled, sold, or transmitted off your device.
QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Lists launchable applications installed on your device so you can select which apps to guard. Used solely to populate the in-app app picker with app names and icons. Your installed app list is never collected, stored remotely, or shared.
SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW
(Display Over Other Apps)		 Allows Pause to present the mindful friction gate screen when a guarded app is opened. Used exclusively to launch the friction intervention screen. Does not capture screenshots, keystrokes, or content from other apps.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE		 Keeps the lightweight on-device app-launch monitor active while Friction Shield, a Focus Session, or an active Schedule is running. Runs locally on-device with a persistent low-priority status notification.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS
(Android 13+)		 Displays the active guard status notification and optional pre-expiry heads-up before a temporary unlock ends. Handled locally by the Android notification system.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Automatically resumes the on-device guard after a device restart if Friction Shield or a Schedule is enabled. Runs locally on boot without network communication.
REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS Optional exemption so the operating system does not prematurely stop the background guard service. Managed directly through Android system battery settings at your discretion.

No Third-Party Sharing: Pause does not sell, rent, or share any device usage information, installed application lists, or personal notes with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

4. Data Deletion and Retention

  • In-App Controls: You can clear your friction history or reset all guarded app rules at any time from the Settings tab inside the app.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling Pause permanently deletes all locally stored rules, focus statistics, reminders, and history from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Pause does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The application operates entirely offline on the user's device without account creation or personal data collection.

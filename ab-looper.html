AB Looper: Video Practice

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

1. Overview

AB Looper: Video Practice (com.universalieapps.ablooper) is a precision A–B video and audio looper designed for choreography, dance, athletics, music, and motor skill drills, developed and published by Universalie Apps.

We believe that your practice sessions, training footage, and personal recordings belong exclusively to you. Your privacy is protected by default through uncompromising architecture:

  • 100% On-Device Processing: All video decoding, 0.1s timeline inspection, zoom inspection, mirror flipping, audio pitch-locked time stretching, and countdown metronome generation execute strictly on your device hardware.
  • Zero Data Collection & Zero Remote Servers: We do not operate remote servers, user accounts, cloud databases, or tracking telemetry. We never collect your name, email, device identifier, IP address, or location.
  • Zero Advertising SDKs: The application contains no advertisements, no banner networks, and no Advertising ID (AD_ID) access.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: Media loaded into AB Looper never leaves your phone or tablet.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All preferences, practice loops, and configuration states are saved locally in private app sandbox storage (SharedPreferences) and are never transmitted:

  • A–B Loop Presets: Custom timestamps (Pin A and Pin B), loop durations, playback speed settings, rep counts, and custom names you choose to save for future drill recall.
  • Practice & Looper Settings: Your configured count-in duration (Off, 3s, 5s), metronome sound style (Digital Beep, Woodblock, Click), haptic pulse toggle, rest pause between loops (0s–5s), auto-speed drill stepping, and Bluetooth audio latency sync offset.
  • Video Inspection Preferences: Screen wake lock preference, default horizontal mirror flip mode, micro-scrub resolution (0.1s, 0.05s, 0.033s), alignment grid style (Rule of Thirds, Crosshairs, Grid + Horizon) and grid opacity.
  • Gesture & Hardware Preferences: Hardware volume button stepping mode and double-tap canvas behavior.
  • Recently Loaded Media Path: The local file URI/path of the last active file if you have enabled the "Auto-Restore Last Media" feature.

3. Device Permissions & Prominent Disclosure

AB Looper requests only the minimum Android permissions strictly required to provide local video playback, audio synthesis, and hands-free drill practice:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
READ_MEDIA_VIDEO
(Android 13+ / API 33+)		 Enables you to browse and select local video files from your device to load into the player. Accessed strictly on-demand when you tap "Choose Video". The video is decoded locally by Android hardware decoders and never uploaded or shared.
READ_MEDIA_AUDIO
(Android 13+ / API 33+)		 Enables you to browse and select local audio tracks and instrumentals to loop for music or rhythm drills. Accessed strictly on-demand when you tap "Choose Audio". The audio file remains entirely local to your device.
READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE
(Android 12 and below, maxSdkVersion=32)		 Enables older Android devices to browse and load user-selected media files for drill practice. Used solely to read the specific video or audio file you select. We do not index or scan your broader files.
VIBRATE Provides tactile haptic pulses synchronized with metronome countdown beeps so you feel the tempo count-in before loop repetition. Controlled strictly in real-time by the local haptic actuator. No sensory or device data is recorded.
WAKE_LOCK / FLAG_KEEP_SCREEN_ON Prevents your phone or tablet screen from dimming or sleeping while practicing drills hands-free. Applied only to the active practice activity window while you are practicing. Releases automatically when exiting.
INTERNET Allows optional external taps (e.g. tapping "Rate on Google Play" or sending an email bug report). The core application functionality does not use network connections and operates 100% offline.

No Third-Party Sharing: Universalie Apps does not sell, license, rent, trade, or share your media files, loop timestamps, practice habits, or device information with any third party, advertiser, analytics provider, or data broker.

4. User Content & Media Privacy Guarantee

Your practice footage may contain personal, proprietary, or sensitive training routines (such as dance routines, martial arts katas, gymnastics choreography, golf swings, or musical performances).

We guarantee that AB Looper does not transmit, upload, back up, or stream your videos or audio to any server. Every frame is decoded, scaled, mirrored, and displayed directly within your device's graphic memory (OpenGL/Vulkan texture buffers).

5. Data Retention and Deletion

  • In-App Clear Storage: You can purge your saved loop presets and clear recently opened media paths at any time from Settings > Presets & Storage > Clear Media History & Presets.
  • Uninstalling the App: Uninstalling AB Looper permanently and irreversibly erases all local application data, loop presets, and user settings from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

AB Looper is designed as a universal utility for athletes, musicians, dancers, students, and coaches. It does not collect personal information from any user, including children under the age of 13, and complies fully with COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act) and GDPR-K standards.

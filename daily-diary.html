Daily Diary

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

1. Overview

Daily Diary (package com.universalieapps.dailydiary) is an offline-first personal journaling and diary application published by Universalie Apps. Your thoughts and memories belong solely to you:

  • No External Tracking or Analytics: We do not operate tracking servers, nor do we integrate third-party analytics or crash-telemetry SDKs.
  • Zero Advertising SDKs: The app contains no ad networks, ad trackers, or advertising ID collection.
  • Local-First Storage: All journal entries, mood logs, custom moods, tags, photos, videos, voice recordings, and file attachments are stored directly on your device.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Journal Entries & Moods: Titles, formatted diary text, timestamps, location labels, tags, favorite/pin states, and custom mood definitions are stored locally in the app's private storage directory (journal_entries.json and custom_moods.json).
  • Media Attachments: Photos, videos, audio clips, and document files you attach to entries remain stored locally on your device.
  • Voice Recordings: Audio recorded with the in-app voice recorder is saved exclusively to your device's local storage.
  • Home Screen Widgets & App Lock: Android home screen widgets (Quick Actions, Notes Feed, and Sticky Note) read strictly from your local journal storage. App Lock uses Android's hardware-backed biometric/device authentication without ever accessing or storing raw biometric data.
  • Local Backups & Exports: User-initiated .zip vault snapshots (entries and media) and .json backups are generated locally on-device and shared only when you explicitly choose to export or share them.

3. Device Permissions

Daily Diary requests only the permissions needed for features you choose to use:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
RECORD_AUDIO Record voice notes directly inside the journal entry composer. Saved locally as audio files on your device. Never streamed or uploaded to developer servers.
ACTION_GET_CONTENT
(Android System Picker)		 Browse and attach photos, videos, audio files, or documents to diary entries and import local backups. Uses Android's privacy-preserving system file/media picker—grants access only to the specific files you select, without broad storage permissions.
INTERNET
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE		 Optional Google Sign-In and Google Drive™ cloud backup & restore. Used exclusively when you sign in with Google to sync or restore your private Drive backup. The app operates 100% offline otherwise.

4. Optional Google Drive™ Cloud Backup

Daily Diary includes an optional cloud backup feature allowing you to back up and restore your journal entries and vault archives using your personal Google Drive™ account.

  • Restricted App Data Scope: The app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata scope, granting access solely to its own hidden application folder (daily_diary_backup.json and daily_diary_vault.zip) in your Google Drive.
  • No Access to Other Drive Files: Daily Diary cannot see, read, modify, or delete any of your other personal Google Drive files or folders.
  • Account Info Displayed Locally: When signed in, your Google display name, email address, and profile photo are displayed locally in the Account & Backup screen so you know which account is connected.
  • Google API Policy Compliance: Daily Diary's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Direct Encrypted Transfer: Backup and restore requests communicate directly between your device and Google's servers over encrypted HTTPS.

5. Data Deletion and Disconnection

  • In-App Delete & Disconnect: Inside Settings → Account & Backup → Account & Data Removal, you can permanently delete all cloud backups stored in Google Drive's appDataFolder, revoke/sever Google account access to this app, and optionally delete all local journal entries on your device.
  • Google Account Security Settings: You can also revoke Daily Diary's access at any time from your Google Account third-party connections settings.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the application permanently removes all locally stored entries, custom moods, and app data from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Daily Diary does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All journal data remains on the user's personal device or in their own personal Google Drive appDataFolder if they choose to enable backup.

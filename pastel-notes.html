Pastel Notes

Developer: Universalie Apps  •  Effective Date: September 25, 2026

1. Overview

Pastel Notes is an offline-first note-taking application published by Universalie Apps. Your privacy is paramount:

  • No External Tracking: We do not operate tracking servers or collect your personal note contents.
  • Zero Advertising SDKs: The app does not contain third-party ad networks, advertising trackers, or device advertising ID collection.
  • Local-First Storage: All notes, checklists, images, and audio recordings are stored directly on your device.

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Notes & Text: Titles, formatting, checklist items, tags, and timestamps are stored in a private local database on your device.
  • Media Attachments: Images and videos attached to notes remain stored in the app's sandboxed local storage.
  • Voice Recordings: Audio recorded with the built-in microphone tool is saved exclusively to your local device storage.

3. Device Permissions

The application only requests runtime permissions required for explicit user features:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
RECORD_AUDIO Voice notes recorder inside the editor. Processed in real-time to save local audio files on device. Never streamed or uploaded to developer servers.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Displays scheduled note reminder notifications. Handled locally by the operating system notification manager.
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Ensures timed reminders trigger accurately. Scheduled locally with the device alarm manager.
USE_BIOMETRIC Biometric lock for protected notes. Handled entirely by Android hardware security. The app never accesses or stores fingerprint/face data.
INTERNET Optional Google Drive cloud backup & stability reporting. Used for user-initiated Google Drive backups and anonymous crash diagnostics.

4. Optional Google Drive™ Cloud Backup

Pastel Notes includes an optional backup feature allowing you to save backup archives directly to your personal Google Drive account.

  • Restricted App Data Scope: The app requests only the drive.appdata scope, which grants access solely to its own hidden backup file inside your personal Google Drive.
  • No Access to Other Files: The app cannot read, view, modify, or delete any of your other personal Google Drive documents or files.
  • Google API Policy Compliance: Pastel Notes' use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Direct Connection: All backup data transfers communicate directly between your device and Google's servers over secure encrypted HTTPS.

5. Diagnostics & Analytics (Firebase Crashlytics & Analytics)

To ensure app stability, detect bugs, and resolve crashes across diverse Android device configurations, Pastel Notes integrates privacy-hardened Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics (provided by Google LLC):

  • Crash Diagnostics: When an unexpected app error occurs, anonymous stack traces, OS versions, and device hardware specs are transmitted to help diagnose and resolve the issue.
  • Anonymous Usage Metrics: Aggregated, non-identifiable feature interactions (such as note creation counts or backup trigger events) help us understand app performance and stability.
  • Advertising ID Disabled: Google Advertising ID (`AD_ID`), hardware SSAID collection, and ad personalization signals are permanently disabled in the application manifest.
  • Zero Content Collection: Neither diagnostic logs nor analytics ever collect, read, or transmit note contents, note titles, voice recordings, attachments, or personal email addresses.
  • Encrypted Transmission: All diagnostic information is transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS connections in compliance with Google Play Developer Policies.

6. Data Deletion and Retention

  • Trash: Deleted notes remain in the in-app Trash until manually emptied or automatically expired after 30 days, which permanently removes all database records and attached media from your device.
  • Cloud Backups: You can delete your backup file at any time from within the app settings or via your Google Account security settings.
  • Uninstalling: Uninstalling the application permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.

7. Children's Privacy

Pastel Notes does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. All note data remains on the user's personal device.

Lifevents

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

1. Overview

Lifevents (published by Universalie Apps) is an unlimited event countdown, milestone tracker, and home screen widget application for Android. Your privacy and data sovereignty are fundamental to our architecture:

  • Offline-First Core: You can create unlimited countdowns, custom recurrence rules, alarms, notes, and widgets completely offline without ever creating an account.
  • Zero Advertising SDKs: Lifevents contains no ad networks, tracking beacons, behavioral profiling, or advertising trackers.
  • Optional Cloud Backup: Cloud synchronization via Google Sign-In is strictly optional. If enabled, your data is stored in your private Google Cloud Firestore partition, protected by strict user-level authorization rules.

2. Data Collection and Usage

  • Countdown & Milestone Details: Event titles, target dates, custom recurrence configurations, display unit settings, milestone notes, and alarms are stored in a private, sandboxed SQLite database (events.db) on your device.
  • Google Account Information (Optional): When you choose to sign in with Google to enable cloud backup, your public Google profile name, email address, and unique user identifier (UID) are processed through Google Play Services / Firebase Authentication solely to establish your private cloud storage partition. We never sell or share this information.
  • Cloud Backup Data (Optional): When cloud sync is active, your countdown events are transmitted via encrypted HTTPS and stored securely in Google Cloud Firestore under users/{userId}/events/. Only you can read, modify, or delete this data.
  • Local JSON Export: You can export and import complete countdown backups to local files via Android's Storage Access Framework (SAF) without internet connectivity.

3. Device Permissions

The application requests only permissions essential to its core countdown, alert, and widget features:

Permission Purpose Data Handling
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM & USE_EXACT_ALARM Triggers punctual alerts and alarms at the exact second a countdown reaches zero or hits a scheduled milestone. Handled locally by Android AlarmManager. Never shared or uploaded.
POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers timely countdown milestone alerts, anniversary reminders, and alarm banners. Managed locally by the Android system notification framework.
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK & FOREGROUND_SERVICE Provides reliable audio playback when a full-screen alarm rings until dismissed. Plays local ringtone or notification sounds entirely on-device.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Reschedules active countdown alarms and refreshes home screen widgets automatically after your device reboots. Executes locally on device startup to re-register alarms with AlarmManager.
USE_BIOMETRIC Optional fingerprint, face, or device PIN lock to secure your countdown events. Processed exclusively by the Android BiometricPrompt hardware security module. The app never accesses or stores biometric data.
VIBRATE & WAKE_LOCK Provides tactile feedback and wakes the screen momentarily when an important countdown alarm triggers. Executed strictly on-device.
INTERNET Powers optional Google Sign-In and secure Cloud Firestore event synchronization. All data transfers communicate directly with Google's Firebase servers over encrypted HTTPS (TLS 1.3).

4. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the app and Google Cloud / Firebase servers are encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Cloud Security Rules: Firestore security rules enforce strict user-level access controls: only requests with authenticated tokens matching your unique Google UID can access your countdown collection.
  • No Third-Party Access: We do not provide access to your countdown data to any third-party advertisers, brokers, or external analytics vendors.

5. User Rights & Complete Data Deletion

You retain full control over your data at all times:

  • In-App Cloud Account Deletion: You can permanently wipe all your cloud-synced countdown data and sever all Google connections directly inside the app by going to Settings > Account & Backup > Delete Account & Cloud Backups. This permanently purges your Firestore documents and deletes your user record.
  • Trash Retention: Deleted countdowns reside in the in-app Trash and are permanently purged after 30 days or immediately upon selecting "Empty Trash".
  • Local Data Removal: Uninstalling the app immediately deletes all local database records, cached images, and preferences from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

Lifevents does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is family-friendly and operates as a standalone utility.
